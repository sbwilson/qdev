# Deferred Work

Items deferred from reviews and implementation, kept out of the story they surfaced in.

**Every entry carries a `Review by:` point** — a story, an epic-planning moment, or a measurable
condition ("the first time the 30 ms boot budget is missed"). Recording an item without one is
what let two items deferred after the story 1.7 review go on to cause both of epic 1's blocking
defects: they were correctly diagnosed, then never looked at again. An item nobody has to revisit
is not deferred, it is forgotten. Resolved entries stay in place rather than being deleted — the ledger is the record of what was
known and when. A keyed entry records its resolution in a `resolved:` line, leaving `summary:` and
`evidence:` as they were written; the older prose entries above strike the summary through and
append a `RESOLVED <date>` note. Do not edit a `summary:` or `evidence:` value to say something
was fixed: a machine reading this file (`bmad-loop-sweep` triages the open entries) then sees a
summary full of markup and an evidence field that contradicts itself.
